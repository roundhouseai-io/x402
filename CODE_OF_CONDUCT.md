# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being kind and taking other people's time seriously;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels;
- giving and accepting review feedback gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for users of the tool;
- saying "I was wrong" without drama.

Behaviour that does not:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- anything you would not want quoted back to you in public.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code review — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@roundhouse.ai**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will respond as they judge appropriate, from a private word through to a permanent ban, and will explain the decision to the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow this in good faith may face consequences decided by other maintainers.
